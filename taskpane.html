    Features

    🎯 Selection lockOnly your selected cells change. Edits that would overwrite data elsewhere come unticked.
    👀 PreviewSee every change before it happens; accept all or some.
    ↩︎ UndoUndo any AI change from the panel or the History tab.
    🧮 Real formulasAnswers are live Excel formulas you can check, not pasted numbers.
    🏷 Named cells“Call this Span” → formulas read =Load_w*Span^2/8.
    📷 Image → cellsPaste a photo of a table, a textbook formula or an error message.
    ✨ Cell memoryClick a cell to see which prompt made it.
    🛠 No ribbon huntingColours, borders, dropdowns, freeze, sort, charts, pivots — just ask.

    Tips

    • Select just your data, not whole columns.
    • One cell selected + “make a table of…” fills empty cells next to it.
    • Be specific about where: “in column D”, “on a new sheet”.
    • Follow-ups work: “make it blue instead”.
    • Only the selected cells and a short summary of the sheet are sent to the AI.
